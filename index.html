TEW03

A monophonic acid synth. Classic 303 squelch (saw/square, resonant filter, accent, slide, drive) plus extra nastiness.

The main page is the synth, a 3×12 step sequencer (16 or 32 steps, accent and slide, key lock), and two drawn LFOs you can drop on any knob. Patterns play the voice and go out as MIDI.

Run modes: Keyboard (play from MIDI), Standalone (loop the selected slot; the DAW UI hides this), and Pattern (hold a key to trigger a pattern).

Effects are chorus, compressor, delay, distortion, EQ, filter, flanger, phaser, and reverb — click to add, drag to reorder, all off until you turn them on. A five-band parametric EQ sits before and after the effects, with the spectrum behind the curve.

VST3 and standalone.

K1X

A kick drum (909 default, plus 808 / 707 / Tight / Hard).

Tune, click, decay, drive, filters, then a parallel FX send (distortion / delay / reverb) and post EQ.

Length and reverb predelay can snap to the DAW tempo.

Standalone Play previews the kick. Export WAV writes a stereo shot including the FX tail.

VST3 and standalone.

5HKR

A perc groove box: eight synth lanes on a 16th-note grid. Not a sample player.

Lanes are CHH, OHH, Crash, Cym, Shaker, Clave, Cow, Rim. The kit menu picks 707 / 808 / 909 / Linn for that lane. Pattern banks, per-lane swing and loop length, then a send into delay, reverb, chorus, flanger, and phaser, plus post EQ and a sidechain duck.

36 factory grooves: bank 1 house/techno, bank 2 latin/afro, bank 3 broken/polyrhythmic.

Hold MIDI C1 for bank 1 pattern 1 (Ableton C0), like TEW03.

VST3 and standalone.

V4JB

An atmosphere and texture instrument. Pads, drones, air, fog. Sister plugin to TEW03 and K1X.

A modular additive synth: up to 512 sine partials per voice, 8 voices. A spectral stage feeds swappable spectral modules, then drive, chorus or phaser, delay, and reverb.

A morph pad blends four macro snapshots by hand, along drawn paths, or as an envelope.

194 factory presets in 16 categories.

VST3 and standalone.
